# Exercises

## 1. Customer propensity

A company universe and a file of current customer ids are available. The target is whether a company becomes a customer of an advertiser. The positive class is a small minority.

Describe how the two classes differ on:

- advertising impacts
- sales range
- province
- office type

Then treat the data and justify the choices. Train a simple baseline and a stronger model on the same split. Choose the decision threshold with a business score: one advertising impact costs 10 monetary units, and winning a customer yields 200.

From the fitted model, describe the preferred customer profile, explain how to score a new company, and show the cases with the highest predicted probability.

## 2. Legal company names

Legal documents mention companies by their legal names, for example `BANCO SANTANDER S.L.`

Write a short proposal that covers:

- the possible ways to approach the problem
- the methodologies that could be used
- which option or options are the most interesting
- why those options are the most appropriate

This exercise is a written proposal. It does not require an implementation.
